📜 City Rules & Roleplay Guidelines

⚠️ All citizens are expected to follow these rules. Failure to follow the rules may result in a warning, jail, kick, or ban depending on the severity of the violation.

01. 🔫 RDM — Random Deathmatch

Killing another player without a valid IC (In-Character) reason or proper roleplay is strictly prohibited.

Punishment: Warning, punishment, or ban depending on the situation.

02. 🚗 VDM — Vehicle Deathmatch

Using a vehicle intentionally to run over, hit, or kill another player without valid roleplay justification is prohibited.

03. 🧠 METAGAMING

Using information obtained OOC (Out of Character) that your character could not realistically know in an IC situation is prohibited.

Example: Using information from Discord, streams, or another player's OOC conversation inside the city.

04. ⚡ POWERGAMING

Using game mechanics, unrealistic advantages, or actions that would not be possible in realistic roleplay to gain an unfair advantage is prohibited.

Example: Performing unrealistic actions simply because the game mechanics allow you to do so.

05. 🕊️ NLR — NEW LIFE RULE

After respawning or dying, you must treat your previous life as forgotten.

You must not return to the previous situation, remember the events surrounding your death, or use information from your previous life.

NLR duration: 30 minutes.

06. ⚔️ REVENGE ROLEPLAY

Revenge RP is permitted only when there is a valid IC reason.

  • Revenge must be roleplayed within 2 days of the original incident.
  • The revenge must remain completely IC.
  • Offensive, abusive, or personal language is not permitted.
  • OOC disputes must never be converted into IC revenge.

07. 🚪 COMBAT LOGGING

Leaving the server while involved in an active RP situation, chase, fight, arrest, robbery, or other scenario is strictly prohibited.

If you experience a genuine technical issue, inform the staff team as soon as possible.

08. 🤝 RESPECT & HARASSMENT

Every player must be treated with respect both IC and OOC.

Discrimination, harassment, personal attacks, or insulting another player based on gender or identity will not be tolerated.

Respect everyone. No exceptions.

09. 👮 COP BAITING

Purposely provoking, teasing, chasing, or unnecessarily annoying police officers just to create a police chase or force an RP situation is prohibited.

Police interactions must have a legitimate roleplay reason.

10. 🏠 SPAWN KILLING

You may not immediately kill, attack, or kidnap a player who has just spawned or logged into the city. Allow the player reasonable time to load into the city and become active.

Recommended waiting time: 30–40 seconds. A valid RP reason is also required.

11. 😨 FEAR RP

Your character must value their life. If you are held at gunpoint or placed in a clearly life-threatening situation, you must behave realistically and show appropriate fear.

Example: You should not casually fight someone who has a weapon pointed directly at you without a realistic reason.

12. 🚓 COP PRESENCE

Players must roleplay as though the city has an active police presence, even when no police officers are currently online.

Do not assume that you can freely commit crimes simply because no PD member is visible.

13. 🌊 FLOW RP

Not every RP situation will go exactly as planned.

If another player makes a mistake or performs minor Fail RP during an ongoing scenario, continue the situation where reasonably possible instead of intentionally destroying the RP.

After the scenario is completed, you may report the incident to the staff team with valid evidence.

14. ⚡ SUPPRESSIVE / WARNING FIRE

Do not randomly shoot or kill players without establishing a valid RP situation.

Before initiating a shootout, players should provide a reasonable warning or escalation where the situation allows it.

Random shooting without RP escalation will be treated as RDM.

15. 🎭 PROPER ROLEPLAY

All players must maintain realistic and immersive roleplay. Your actions should be based on what a real person could reasonably do in the same situation.

Avoid unrealistic actions, unnecessary arguments, troll RP, and breaking character.

🛡️ SAFE ZONES

Criminal activities, weapon usage, kidnapping, intentional violence, and hostile RP are prohibited inside designated Safe Zones.

Police Department
EMS Department
News HQ
Mechanic Shed
Taxi Department
Dealerships
City Hall
Side Job Locations

Plus any other locations officially designated as Safe Zones by the staff team.

🚗 CDM — CAR DEATHMATCH

Intentionally crashing, ramming, or repeatedly hitting another player's vehicle without a valid RP reason is considered Car Deathmatch (CDM).

Unnecessary vehicle collisions and using vehicles to damage or disable another player's vehicle are prohibited.
